# Security policy

Thank you for helping keep Parakaleo and the people who use it safe.

If you've found a security problem, email **hello@parakaleoinc.com** with what you found, how to reproduce it, and how we can reach you. Please don't open a public issue for a vulnerability.

We'll confirm we got your report and look into it. Give us a reasonable chance to fix the problem before you share it publicly, and please don't access, change or delete data that isn't yours while you test.

This policy covers parakaleoinc.com, portal.parakaleoinc.com and the Parakaleo Drive app. Our machine-readable contact is at https://parakaleoinc.com/.well-known/security.txt.
