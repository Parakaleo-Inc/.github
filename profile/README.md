# Parakaleo Inc.

**Software that turns what a vehicle already knows into a clear Safety Score.**

Parakaleo Inc. is a Delaware corporation. We make software only. We don't sell, ship or install hardware.

The technology that could help prevent road deaths already exists, but most of it sits inside expensive systems on newer, premium vehicles. We think that's backwards. So we're building a way for far more vehicles to benefit, starting with something we can deliver honestly today: a Safety Score you can read, check and act on.

## Available today: Safety Intelligence

Safety Intelligence observes and scores. It reads what happened, explains it and keeps a record. It never changes a vehicle's behavior.

| Product | What it does | Price |
|---|---|---|
| **Parakaleo Engine** | Software that runs in the vehicle and reads a supported radar the vehicle already has. Needs a vehicle from 2010 or newer with a supported radar, plus parts you source yourself against our published specification. | $15 per vehicle per month |
| **Parakaleo Mobile** | Uses the sensors in a driver's phone through the Parakaleo Drive app. Nothing to buy for the vehicle and nothing to install in the car. | $20 per vehicle per month |

Accounts, vehicles and billing are handled in the licensing portal at [portal.parakaleoinc.com](https://portal.parakaleoinc.com). Our services are open to customers worldwide.

## Planned: Active Vehicle Protection

Active Vehicle Protection would be software that helps avoid collisions. It's on our roadmap and nothing more. It isn't certified, we haven't started certification, and we may never get there. It isn't available, and nothing we sell today depends on it. We'd rather say that plainly than let a roadmap read like a product.

## How we work

- **Honest about what it is.** A score is a score. We don't call it an intervention.
- **Access, not exclusivity.** Not tied to one brand. Compatibility depends on a vehicle's age, radar and CAN access, and, in some cases, the availability of a phone.
- **Private by design.** No advertising trackers, and analytics load only if you accept them. Drivers can delete their data whenever they like.
- **Open about progress.** Our [progress log](https://parakaleoinc.com/progress.html) records what has been done, and only that.
